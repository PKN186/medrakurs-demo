## Семейная прогулка — точный промпт

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape campaign photograph for a modern Russian multidisciplinary medical-center website concept. Create ONE wide cinematic editorial photograph, landscape 16:9. A candid father around 40 and his daughter around 9 walking together outside on a clear gentle autumn morning, relaxed ordinary people, real subtle facial expressions and natural skin, quiet joy in everyday movement. Subjects occupy the RIGHT HALF of the frame, shown from waist up, heads fully visible, child and father looking at each other, not at camera. Natural dark foliage and a modern park path, no recognizable location, no clinic or medical equipment. The LEFT 48 percent is very dark softly out-of-focus foliage with lots of negative space for large white website typography to be added later in code. Soft side/back lighting with a subtle warm coral accent on the father's casual rust-red overshirt, natural green and charcoal surroundings, crisp fine detail with an understated photographic grain. Premium contemporary health brand advertising, energetic and human rather than hospital stock photography, credible not glossy plastic. No visible text, no lettering, no logos, no watermarks, no user interface. No medical uniforms, no treatments, no before-and-after implication. Overall dark natural background must allow readable white text in the left half.
